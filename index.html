Privacy and YouTube API information

This app does not operate a server for your music or keep a cloud library. Imported local audio stays in your browser memory. The app may cache its code offline on your device. Using YouTube services sends search terms and the API key you enter to Google, and embedded YouTube playback shares standard player and device data with Google. Google may collect cookies, device information and playback data under its Privacy Policy. Your API key is not stored by this page after closing it. By enabling YouTube features, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. This is an open source prototype for personal use; there is no developer-operated user account or backend.
